# Business exceptions — owner decision sheet

Status: **proposals for review, not client-approved correction rules**. No historical correction, void, backdating, automatic closing or new permission is implemented in Phase 4. Existing approved visit/duty/assignment/follow-up rules remain in force.

Keep the staff experience simple: report a mistake, describe what happened, and let an authorized reviewer decide. Do not ask staff to edit timestamps or understand audit machinery. Until a policy and implementation are approved, do not use direct SQL, delete a record or enter a fabricated outcome to make a report look correct. An owner-approved private exception log is proposed; no new log/service or message to another person is created by this work.

## Minimum future correction record — proposed, not implemented

One linked record would contain the original record ID/version; requester; claimed change; reason/evidence reference; approving/rejecting staff identity; original and proposed effective values; server submission/decision time; status. Approval must be permission-checked, versioned and transactional with its audit event. Show staff a plain result: Pending review / Approved / Rejected. Original evidence remains readable. Reports must distinguish recorded totals from approved corrections and identify the report generation time.

This is a design proposal only, not a migration or work authorization. The owner should first choose which exceptions need support. Avoid a general-purpose history editor.
