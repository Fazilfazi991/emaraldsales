# Owner demonstration walkthrough

Use only the fictional workspace at http://127.0.0.1:3100/ . Accounts and random passwords are in `development/.local/phase3-demo-accounts.json`. Keep that file private. Use Ahmed and the manager in different browser profiles/private windows.

## Executive — about five minutes

1. Sign in as `ahmed@local.invalid`. **Your working day** is the starting screen; **Today** returns here.
2. Press **Start duty**. Duty timing is separate from visits.
3. Press **Search shops**. Enter **Al Noor**. Open the matching building-materials shop. Read the contact and earlier visits.
4. Press **Check in to this shop**. Allow location if desired; unavailable location is recorded honestly.
5. Press **Check out**. Enter a short answer to **What did you discuss?**, such as “Customer requested cement prices and delivery availability.” Keep **Regular Visit** or choose another outcome. Extra order details are optional.
6. Press **Check Out · Save Visit**. Look for **Visit completed and saved.** and the completed record in history.
7. Press **Add follow-up**, choose today's date, and save. The item is separate from the completed visit. An optional follow-up date during checkout is an alternative; do not add both unless two independent follow-ups are intended.
8. Return to **Today**. Press **Start break**, then **Return to duty**, then **End duty**. Each action confirms success.
9. Refresh, then sign out and sign in again. Find Al Noor: the visit/follow-up remain. Duty history retains start, break, return and end.

For an unconfirmed save, keep the form open, reconnect and use **Retry save**. Do not create a second record to compensate. The same operation key is retained for retry. Offline storage across reloads is not offered.

## Manager — about five minutes

1. Sign in as `manager@local.invalid`. **Daily activity** defaults to today in UAE time. **Overview** returns here.
2. Read the four totals and **Staff activity**. **Duty now** is current attendance status. Recorded sessions and completed visits explicitly use selected dates.
3. Choose Ahmed under **Sales executive**, then **Show activity**, or press his name in Staff activity. **Today** resets dates while retaining the executive selection.
4. Use **Reports and downloads ↓**. Choose **Follow-ups**. Pending items through the end date include overdue work; choose **Selected dates only** for that scope.
5. Press a shop name to read its full shared history. Visits show their actual executives; follow-ups retain their own owners and lifecycle.
6. Return to Overview. Choose dates/executive and **Show activity**. Choose **Visits**, **Duty and breaks**, or **Follow-ups**, then **Excel** or **PDF**. Downloads identify dates, staff, generation time and relevant totals. Excel duty exports include **Break history**, one row per interval.
7. Refresh and sign out/in. Saved activity/history remain. Filters reset to today/all staff on fresh login; records persist.

## Presentation notes

All customers, accounts and activity are synthetic. Names exercise Arabic, Malayalam and accented Latin; a client speaker should approve spelling/layout. Browser emulation does not replace actual-phone and first-time-human acceptance.
